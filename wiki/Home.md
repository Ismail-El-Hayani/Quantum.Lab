## About

Physics Playground transforms abstract physics concepts into interactive experiments. Each module is a self-contained "laboratory" with:

1. **Interactive simulations** — Real-time parameter manipulation
2. **Gamified challenges** — Guided puzzles that teach through discovery
3. **Progress tracking** — XP, levels, and per-module completion

The curriculum follows a logical progression: start with quantum fundamentals (QHO, hydrogen, spin), move through solid-state physics (Kronig-Penney, bands, Fermi surface), and conclude with device applications (semiconductors, junctions, lasers, magnetism).
