# Modules Overview

The Physics Playground contains 16 modules (00–15), each focusing on a core topic in quantum or solid-state physics.

---

## Module 00 — From Crystal to Quantum (Intro)

A 3D zoom journey through a silicon crystal — from 1 cm down to the quantum realm. Fire photons, watch electrons dance, and discover why solid-state physics matters.

- **Tech:** Three.js 3D rendering, photon beam interaction
- **Challenges:** Guided tour with interactive stops

---

## Module 01 — Quantum Harmonic Oscillator

Drag energy levels, build wavefunctions, and match quantum patterns. Tune a virtual laser and observe how the oscillator responds.

- **Simulation:** Real-time wavefunction plotting with Plotly
- **Challenges:** Wave puzzle, energy ladder, laser tuner

---

## Module 02 — Hydrogen Atom

Build orbitals from quantum numbers (n, l, m). Solve spectral mysteries by identifying emission lines.

- **Simulation:** 3D volumetric orbital visualization
- **Challenges:** Orbital builder, spectral detective, electron config

---

## Module 03 — Spin-1/2 & Quantum Measurement

Navigate the Bloch sphere, predict measurement outcomes, and build quantum circuits. Stern-Gerlach experiment simulation.

- **Simulation:** Bloch sphere visualization
- **Challenges:** Bloch navigator, Stern-Gerlach, gate puzzles

---

## Module 04 — Kronig-Penney Model

Design a periodic potential, watch the bandgap open, and engineer your material's electronic properties.

- **Simulation:** Band structure dispersion curves
- **Challenges:** Crystal designer, bandgap builder, wave packet

---

## Module 05 — Energy Bands & Density of States

Hunt the Fermi level across different materials. Explore how dimensionality affects the density of states.

- **Simulation:** E-k diagrams, DOS curves
- **Challenges:** Fermi hunter, material designer, dimension explorer

---

## Module 06 — Fermi Surface & Temperature

Freeze metals, smear Fermi surfaces, and race against temperature. 3D Fermi surface explorer.

- **Simulation:** 3D Fermi surface visualization
- **Challenges:** Temperature race, 3D explorer, metal detector

---

## Module 07 — Electrical Conductivity

Build circuits, race electrons through materials, and discover what makes a superconductor.

- **Simulation:** Drift velocity, resistivity controls
- **Challenges:** Circuit builder, drift race, superconductor quest

---

## Module 08 — Superconductivity

Explore zero resistance below Tc, the Meissner effect, BCS gap, and d-wave symmetry.

- **Simulation:** Temperature-dependent resistivity, gap plots
- **Challenges:** Tc estimator, isotope puzzle, gap lab

---

## Module 09 — Intrinsic Semiconductors

Tune temperature, bandgap energy, and effective mass. Track intrinsic carrier concentration, Fermi level position, and conductivity.

- **Simulation:** ni(T) plots, conductivity vs temperature
- **Challenges:** ni estimator, build Eg formula, conductivity lab

---

## Module 10 — Doped Semiconductors

Add impurities to shift the Fermi level, switch between n-type and p-type, and explore compensated doping.

- **Simulation:** Carrier concentration vs doping, mobility
- **Challenges:** Doping quiz, compensated builder, mobility lab

---

## Module 11 — Junctions & Devices

From p-n junction IV curves to solar cells and LEDs. Device physics in action with tunable parameters.

- **Simulation:** IV curves, band diagrams
- **Challenges:** IV tracer, solar quiz, LED lab

---

## Module 12 — Optics & Dispersion

Complex refractive index, thin-film Airy interference, Fresnel coefficients, Brewster angle, and double-slit partial coherence.

- **Simulation:** Multi-tab optical playground
- **Challenges:** 3D photon playground, material response, thin-film Airy, double-slit coherence

---

## Module 13 — Laser Physics

Stimulated vs spontaneous emission, population inversion, cavity modes, and direct/indirect bandgap semiconductors for telecom lasers.

- **Simulation:** Cavity mode spectrum, gain curves
- **Challenges:** Direct/indirect identification, stimulated vs spontaneous, cavity designer

---

## Module 14 — Magnetism

Dia-, para-, and ferromagnetism. Curie-Weiss law, Ising lattice Monte Carlo, hysteresis loops, and giant magnetoresistance.

- **Simulation:** Ising lattice, hysteresis plotter
- **Challenges:** Ising lattice, hysteresis, ordering match

---

## Module 15 — Thermal Properties

Debye T³ law, electronic specific heat (γT), Wiedemann-Franz law, and thermal expansion.

- **Simulation:** Cv vs T plots, Lorenz number
- **Challenges:** Metal or insulator, Cv builder, heat capacity lab
