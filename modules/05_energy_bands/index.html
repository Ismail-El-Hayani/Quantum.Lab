📖 Lab Notes
The Density of States, \( g(E) \), describes the number of available electronic states per unit energy per unit volume.

1. 3D Bulk: \( g(E) \propto \sqrt{E - E_c} \) — parabolic dispersion near the conduction-band edge.
2. 2D Layer (Quantum Well): \( g(E) \) is a step-function — constant for each sub-band.
3. 1D Wire (Quantum Wire): \( g(E) \propto 1/\sqrt{E - E_c} \) — Van Hove singularities at sub-band edges.
4. Carbon Allotropes: Graphene → linear \( g(E) \propto |E| \); CNT → spiky Van Hove peaks; Fullerene → discrete delta-like spikes.
