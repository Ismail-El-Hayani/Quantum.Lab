✨ Why does this matter?

The hydrogen atom is the template for all atomic structure. Its Schrödinger solution gives the orbital shapes that organize the periodic table. The spectral lines (Lyman, Balmer, Paschen) encode the energy ladder. Every spectral observation of a star begins here.

$$\psi_{nlm}(r,\theta,\phi) = R_{nl}(r)Y_{lm}(\theta,\phi), \quad E_n = -\frac{13.6\ \text{eV}}{n^2}$$
