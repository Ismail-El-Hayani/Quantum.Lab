B. Theory — Why is spin quantized?

Unlike orbital angular momentum which comes from spatial motion, spin is an intrinsic property of the electron — it has no classical analogue. The Stern-Gerlach experiment (1922) proved it is quantized: a beam of silver atoms splits into exactly two paths, never three, never a continuum.

Pauli matrices:

$$\sigma_x = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \sigma_y = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -i \\ i & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \sigma_z = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$$

Spin operator: $\vec{S} = \frac{\hbar}{2}\vec{\sigma}$

Any pure state:

$$|\psi\rangle = \cos\frac{\theta}{2}|\uparrow_z\rangle + e^{i\phi}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}|\downarrow_z\rangle$$

Expectation value: $\langle\vec{S}\rangle = \frac{\hbar}{2}(\sin\theta\cos\phi, \sin\theta\sin\phi, \cos\theta)$ — this is the arrow on the Bloch sphere.

Measurement Postulate
When you measure Sz, the state collapses to either |↑z⟩ or |↓z⟩ with probabilities |cos(θ/2)|² and |sin(θ/2)|². After the measurement, the state is the result — not a mixture. This is why you cannot measure Sx and Sz simultaneously: measuring one destroys information about the other.

The commutation relation $[S_x, S_y] = i\hbar S_z$ (and cyclic permutations) is the mathematical root of uncertainty. It means the spin operators do not share eigenstates — there is no state that is simultaneously an eigenstate of Sx and Sz.

Electron Spin Resonance (ESR) — Resonance Sweep

In a magnetic field B, the spin precesses at Larmor frequency f = gμ_BB/h ≈ 28 GHz/T. Microwave radiation at this frequency flips the spin — principle behind ESR spectrometers, atomic clocks, and NV-center magnetometers.

Nuclear Magnetic Resonance — Larmor Frequency vs Field

Protons in water are tiny magnets. In MRI, a strong field aligns them; a radio pulse flips them; relaxation emits a signal. The ¹H Larmor frequency at 3T is 128 MHz — the basis of all clinical MRI.

Quantum Gates — State Evolution on Bloch Sphere

A qubit is any two-level quantum system. Single-qubit gates are rotations on the Bloch sphere. H-Z-H sends |0⟩ → |+⟩ → |−⟩ → |0⟩. Two-qubit gates (CNOT) create entanglement — the resource behind quantum speedup.

🌌

21-cm Line — Hyperfine Transition in Interstellar Hydrogen

Neutral hydrogen fills interstellar space. The hyperfine interaction between proton and electron spins creates a tiny splitting ΔE = 5.874 μeV. The 1420.4 MHz transition maps galactic structure and probes the Epoch of Reionization.

# spin_engine.py — Spin-1/2 expectation values & measurement
import numpy as np

# Pauli matrices
sx = np.array([[0,1],[1,0]])
sy = np.array([[0,-1j],[1j,0]])
sz = np.array([[1,0],[0,-1]])

# State on Bloch sphere
theta = np.pi/3
phi = np.pi/4
psi = np.array([np.cos(theta/2),
                np.sin(theta/2)*np.exp(1j*phi)])

# Expectation values
Ex = psi.conj() @ sx @ psi
Ey = psi.conj() @ sy @ psi
Ez = psi.conj() @ sz @ psi

# Larmor precession
g, muB, hbar = 2.0023, 5.788e-5, 1  # eV/T, natural units
B = 1.0  # Tesla
omega = g * muB * B / hbar  # rad/s
