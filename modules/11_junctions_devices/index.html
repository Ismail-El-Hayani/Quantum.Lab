✨ Why does this matter?

A p-n junction creates a built-in potential barrier from the diffusion of holes and electrons. Forward bias lowers the barrier, allowing exponential current flow. Reverse bias widens the depletion region, blocking current. Add photocurrent and the same device becomes a solar cell: absorbed photons generate electron-hole pairs that are swept across the junction by the built-in field. Zener diodes exploit heavy doping to make the depletion so thin that electrons tunnel quantum-mechanically through the barrier at precise reverse voltages, giving stable voltage references. MOSFETs replace the junction with a gate-controlled inversion channel, making the ideal switch for digital logic.
