From Crystal to Quantum

The Zoom Journey
Zoom from a macroscopic silicon crystal down through the atomic lattice, past energy bands, to a single atom and into the quantum spin realm. Experience the transition from the classical world to the quantum realm.

Journey Across 14 Orders of Magnitude

This module explores the transition from the classical regime to quantum mechanics by zooming through five distinct spatial scales — from a macroscopic silicon crystal down to quantum spin states. Each scale reveals different physics, and together they tell the story of how quantum behaviour emerges from the atomic structure of matter.

Macro Lattice Cluster Atom Spin
🔬 Macro (1 cm)

The crystal appears continuous. Classical optics govern light-matter interaction: reflection, transmission, absorption.

🔷 Lattice (nm)

The diamond-cubic arrangement of silicon atoms becomes visible. Periodicity creates allowed and forbidden energy bands.

🔗 Cluster (Å)

Individual tetrahedral bonds between atoms emerge. Bonding and anti-bonding states define the electronic structure.

The Bohr model and electron orbitals. The bandgap originates from discrete atomic energy levels splitting in the crystal field.

🌀 Spin (fm)

The deepest scale — electron spin and Zeeman splitting in magnetic fields. The transition from spatial structure to purely quantum degrees of freedom.

Key Concepts

Crystal Periodicity

Atoms arranged in a repeating lattice create a periodic potential that determines the electronic band structure via Bloch's theorem.

Bandgap Engineering

The energy gap between valence and conduction bands determines whether a material acts as insulator, semiconductor, or conductor.

Light-Matter Interaction

Photons with energy above the bandgap are absorbed, creating electron-hole pairs. Below-gap photons transmit through the crystal.

Quantum Emergence

Classical behaviour at the macro scale emerges from quantum statistics averaged over ~10²³ atoms — the correspondence principle in action.

🎓 Theory — From Crystal Lattice to Quantum Confinement

Each zoom level reveals a new physical regime. Here is the theory behind each scale.

1. Macro Scale — Classical Continuum

At centimeter scale, silicon behaves as a continuous solid. Its properties — density, thermal conductivity, elastic modulus — are averages over billions of atoms. Quantum effects are completely hidden by statistical averaging. This is the domain of classical physics.

2. Lattice Scale — The Crystal Structure

Zoom to 5 Å and the discrete nature of matter appears. Silicon has a diamond cubic structure with lattice constant a = 5.43 Å. Each atom bonds covalently to four nearest neighbors. The periodic potential of the lattice gives rise to energy bands.

$$a_{\text{Si}} = 5.43\,\text{Å}, \qquad \text{Coordination} = 4, \qquad \text{Bond angle} = 109.5^\circ$$
3. Cluster Scale — Finite Size Effects

At 2 Å we see a small cluster of 5 atoms in tetrahedral arrangement. With only a handful of atoms, the electronic states begin to feel the boundaries. This is the onset of quantum confinement — the energy levels start to discretize as the crystal size approaches the exciton Bohr radius.

4. Atomic Scale — Bohr Model and Energy Bands

Zoom to 100 pm and we see a single silicon atom with 14 electrons. The Bohr model shows electrons orbiting in shells: 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p². The valence band (3s, 3p) and conduction band appear as the discrete atomic levels broaden into bands through the crystal's periodic potential. The band gap Eg = 1.12 eV determines silicon's semiconducting behavior.

$$E_g = 1.12\,\text{eV}, \qquad \lambda_{\text{edge}} = \frac{hc}{E_g} \approx 1107\,\text{nm}$$
5. Spin Scale — Pauli Exclusion and Spin Splitting

At 10 pm, each energy level splits into two: spin-up (↑) and spin-down (↓). This is the Zeeman-like splitting due to the electron's intrinsic magnetic moment. The Pauli exclusion principle allows at most two electrons per orbital — the foundation of the periodic table and all of chemistry.

$$\text{Pauli: } \Delta E_{\text{spin}} = g\mu_B B, \qquad g \approx 2.0023, \qquad \mu_B = 5.788 \times 10^{-5}\,\text{eV/T}$$
