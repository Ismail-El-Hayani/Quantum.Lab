✨ Why does this matter?

Heat capacity tells how energy is stored, thermal conductivity how it is transported, and thermal expansion how the lattice responds. Phonons dominate in insulators; in metals, electrons add a linear γT term. At low T, insulators follow Cv ∝ T³ (Debye) while metals show Cv = βT³ + γT. The Wiedemann-Franz law links thermal and electrical transport: κ/σ ∝ T.

$$C_V \approx \beta T^3 + \gamma T \qquad \kappa = \kappa_{\text{phonon}} + \kappa_{\text{electron}} \qquad \frac{\kappa}{\sigma T} = L_0$$
