Electrical Conductivity

Drift, scatter, conduct. Watch electrons move through a crystal lattice under electric field.

🔬 Drude Electron Drift
Electrons drift through a crystal lattice. Yellow flash = scattering event. Purple arrow = E-field. Cyan vector = average drift velocity.
⚡ Drift Velocity — v_d = eEτ/m*. Linear with field; saturation at high E.
📈 Resistivity vs T — Bloch-Grüneisen: ρ ∝ T⁵ (low T) → ρ ∝ T (high T).
🔌 I-V Curves — Ohmic (linear) vs Diode (exponential). Log scale highlights turn-on.
🎯 Mean Free Path — Matthiessen's rule: 1/λ_total = 1/λ_ph + 1/λ_imp.
🧲 Hall Effect — Sign of V_H distinguishes n-type (negative) vs p-type (positive).
🧊 Ioffe–Regel Criterion — k_F λ > 1 marks the metallic limit. Below → localization.
🔥 Wiedemann–Franz Law — L = κ/(σT). Approaches 2.44×10⁻⁸ WΩ/K² for Fermi liquids.
