✨ Why does this matter?

A laser needs population inversion and an optical cavity. Stimulated emission produces coherent photons matching the energy gap. The cavity selects discrete modes λm = 2nL/m. For lasing, a mode must lie within the gain spectrum.

$$h\nu = E_g \qquad \lambda_m = \frac{2nL}{m} \qquad \text{Gain} \propto N_2 - N_1$$
