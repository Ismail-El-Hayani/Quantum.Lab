✨ Why does this matter?

The Kronig-Penney model is the training gym of solid-state physics. Its exact transcendental equation shows how periodic potentials split free-electron parabolas into bands separated by forbidden gaps. At the Brillouin zone boundary k = ±π/a, Bragg reflection creates standing waves — one peaked at barriers (higher E), one at wells (lower E) — and the energy difference is the bandgap. Every semiconductor device, LED, and solar cell is engineered by tuning this gap.

$$\cos(ka) = \cos(\alpha w)\cosh(\beta b) + \frac{\beta^2 - \alpha^2}{2\alpha\beta}\sin(\alpha w)\sinh(\beta b)$$
