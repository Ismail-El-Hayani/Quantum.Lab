✨ Why does this matter?

The optical response of a material is governed by its dielectric function ε(ω) = ε₁ + iε₂. The Drude model captures free-electron response: a plasma frequency ωp marks the boundary between reflecting (ω < ωp) and transmitting (ω > ωp) behavior. The complex refractive index n + iκ encodes phase velocity and absorption.

$$\epsilon(\omega) = 1 - \frac{\omega_p^2}{\omega^2 + i\gamma\omega}, \qquad R = \frac{(n-1)^2 + \kappa^2}{(n+1)^2 + \kappa^2}$$
